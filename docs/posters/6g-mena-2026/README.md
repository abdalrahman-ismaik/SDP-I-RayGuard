# RayGuard — 6G MENA poster review draft

Updated 30 September 2026. **Version 6 is a draft for Prof. Naoufel and the team to
review and improve.** Advisor approval, final print size and conference submission
are not yet recorded.

The poster introduces single-baggage concealed-threat detection and the proposed
multi-package approach. Version 6 adds paired full-scan/detail figures, a larger
component-grounding example and three numbered close-ups. The supplied author list,
editable text/diagrams and justified prose are retained. Source scan annotations
remain raster images, with reversible PowerPoint crops and editable callouts.

## Review and revision

Review the scientific scope, methods, figure clarity, interpretation and credits.
Use the PPTX for direct revisions; export a matching PDF when the editable file
changes. Record substantive revisions and update the current links and evidence
record together. Earlier working drafts and original source PDFs remain local.

Published AP and mIoU results are distinct metrics from separate papers. The local
RayGuard example is a training-image demonstration; its confidence is not accuracy.
Multi-package fusion and its performance remain proposed. Source-image resolution
limits the detail visible in enlarged crops; no generated scan details were added.
