### Practical order

1. **Target ZU SRC 2027** as the most suitable upcoming local student venue. Confirm whether the
   project meets its student-author rules and write from verified, reproducible results.
2. ISDIA and ICISAS close on **15 November**, with no cutoff time/time zone confirmed here. They
   are realistic only if a complete paper already exists and the supervisor approves. Do not
   rush unsupported claims or violate the ICISAS AI policy.
3. Keep AICCONF as a candidate while verifying its IEEE conference record and exact proceedings
   route. MARIS-AI is geographically local but substantively mismatched.

The project is a prototype effort. Do not present a planned fine-tuning pipeline, synthetic test,
empty detections, or the dismantled-scan collection as validated single-scan benchmark results.
Preserve the published IEDXray test split; do not expose private lab scans or credentials. Agree
authorship, supervisor approval, data permissions and any submission exclusivity before submission.

### ISDIA 2027 invitation

The supplied email advertises a hybrid Dubai event on 9–10 January 2027, a 15 November paper
deadline and Springer LNNS / Scopus publication. The official call confirms the deadline and
venue/modality and describes LNNS submission for accepted, registered and presented papers.
Indexing remains conditional. The official site describes UOWD as host and IRC as organizer/
partner, whereas the forwarded email calls UOWD the organizer. Clarify this and submission
eligibility directly through official institutional channels before paying or submitting.

### CVCI 2027 invitation — international, not a local opportunity

The supplied email advertises 22–24 January 2027 in Osaka, a **5 December 2026** submission
deadline and SPIE proceedings indexed by Ei Compendex and Scopus. The [current official CVCI
site](http://www.cvci.net/) instead lists **30 November 2026** as the full-paper deadline and
**15 December 2026** for acceptance notification; it says accepted, registered and presented
papers are published in its SPIE proceedings and included in the SPIE Digital Library, and the
site claims indexing by Ei Compendex/Scopus. The invitation's deadline conflicts with the live
official dates page: use **30 November** unless the committee confirms otherwise in writing.
This is an overseas event, so it is recorded separately from the UAE shortlist. Treat all
publication/indexing statements as conference claims subject to publisher/indexer decisions.
