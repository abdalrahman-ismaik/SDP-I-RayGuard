## How to register

The [attendee form](https://eu-ems.com/register.asp?event_id=4979), also supplied by a team
member on 24 September, lists four stages: **Contact Information → Delegate Information →
Confirm Booking → Registration Complete**. For SDP students, suggested profile entries are:

| Field | Suggested entry |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Khalifa University |
| Position | Undergraduate Student – Computer Science |
| Organisation type | Select the closest university/academic option actually offered; dropdown choices have not been verified |

Enter your correct title, name, country, phone and email directly in the form using normal
capitalization. A university email is recommended. Required fields are marked with an asterisk.
Select **Attending the event** when registering yourself. If booking for someone else, enter
your own details as the main contact, select the corresponding option and add their delegate
details later, with their permission. The form also allows multiple delegates on later pages.

Review the details, complete the booking and retain the confirmation privately. Do not put
contact details or booking confirmations in the repository. Viewing or starting the form does
not complete registration; it supplies no poster template, poster-upload route or deadline.
