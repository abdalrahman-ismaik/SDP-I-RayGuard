# Free online tools for the RayGuard use-case videos

## Flow follow-up: free duration and continuity

Rechecked official help on 19 October 2026. The
[credit table](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en) gives non-subscribers
50 daily credits without daily rollover. Calculated theoretical maxima, assuming the listed
models/rates are available and no credits spent elsewhere: Omni Flash 360p, 70 seconds/day
(seven 10-second clips, 49 credits); Omni Flash 720p, 32 seconds/day (four 8-second clips,
48 credits). Conditional Veo Lite: 40 seconds/day; Fast: 16 seconds/day. These are raw
generated seconds, not usable edited footage; successful unwanted takes consume the budget.
Charging is per output, so select one output per attempt. Free video access may be restricted
around 18:00-21:00 UAE time. No free non-subscriber 1080p upscaling is listed.

Access caveat: the [getting-started guide](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333?hl=en)
lists subscription requirements, while the credit page explicitly covers non-subscribers
and the [model guide](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en) says all users
can access Omni. Confirm actual models and costs in the account; not every model is promised
on Free. Current standalone lengths reach 10 seconds on Omni and 8 seconds on Veo.

For the airport videos, recommend 720p Omni if available, one output, and a fixed visual
style. Prepare clean character/baggage/checkpoint references first. Follow
[Google's guidance](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en): consistent
ingredients, compatible text/image instructions and start/end frames. Veo ingredients
currently require 8-second clips, qualifying the earlier generic six-second prompts.
Project recommendation: one action and one camera move per clip; add readable captions,
scan graphics and actual GUI evidence later. Keep proposed multi-package results labelled.
[Save frames and build scenes](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16935718) to continue
shots and arrange/trim the sequence. No account generation/export was executed.

## Requested scope

The owner reports Prof. Naoufel wants two airport-security concept videos: (1) component
evidence distributed across one passenger's bags; (2) component evidence across bags carried
by different people. Motion graphics or AI-generated realistic characters are acceptable.
No video duration or resolution is specified in that report. This supplements T37 planning;
it does not reassign Hardik/Fangzhou's separate work/approaches video from F08.

Show a proposed IED-detection use case with abstract component evidence and human review.
Do not present generated X-rays, boxes or scores as measured model predictions. For the
different-passenger scenario, make the illustrative case linkage an explicit external input;
RayGuard does not establish passenger identity or infer coordination from the images.

## Options with weaker free access

- [Runway pricing](https://runway.com/pricing) advertises 125 **one-time** free credits and
  selected models; watermark-free output is listed in Standard. Useful for tests, not a
  dependable recurring free production budget.
- [Adobe Firefly](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/apps/generative-ai/generative-credits-faq.html)
  offers limited complimentary premium generations; the actual account allowance must be
  checked. Do not assume enough free clips for both videos and revisions.
- [Pika's current pricing](https://pika.art/pricing) lists **0 monthly credits** on Free,
  with purchased credit packs. Older FAQ/search snippets describe different allowances;
  do not recommend it as a dependable zero-cost generator based on those older figures.

## Proposed production route

For a dependable zero-budget result, build two 45-60 second motion-graphics explainers in
Canva and assemble/narrate in Clipchamp. Duration is a recommendation, not an advisor deadline.
Use consistent passenger/bag labels and abstract X-ray-style evidence panels. Add all labels,
arrows and UI overlays manually so the explanation stays readable and consistent.

For a realistic variant, generate only short airport/passenger/staff shots in Flow or Bing,
then add the controlled evidence/GUI sequence in the editor. Start with one short generation
and one export test; character continuity and exact scanner displays have not been tested.

Video 1: one passenger, several bags, separate scans, evidence collected into one supplied
case, proposed combined-risk alert, officer review. Video 2: different passengers/bags,
separate scans, externally supplied illustrative case linkage, combined evidence view and
officer review. Show conceptual association, without device assembly instructions.

Use a visible label such as **Illustrative scenario - proposed multi-package capability**.
Any actual GUI recording must retain real model/task identity and distinguish existing
single-image execution from proposed fusion. Do not label an empty detection as safe.

[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) is an optional free source for neutral airport
establishing shots. Its license prohibits depicting identifiable people in a bad light or
implying endorsement: use fictional/animated characters for the suspicious-case narrative.

No accounts were created, generation credits consumed, media uploaded, videos generated
or exports tested during this research. Next: select a style, prepare two storyboards and
test one representative scene/export before building the full videos.
